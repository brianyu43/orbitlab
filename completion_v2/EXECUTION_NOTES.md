# Execution amendments and interpretation boundaries

- All new artifacts are under `completion_v2/`. Historical run/report files were preserved. Four followup manifest differences existed before this execution; the final audit compares both the mismatch set and their starting hashes.
- MPS was hidden in the restricted execution environment. Authorized host execution confirmed availability, and numerical forward/gradient probes preceded confirmation acceleration. The original generation waiting driver was replaced by a host waiting driver without interrupting the ongoing CPU development training. This was an execution amendment, not deletion or reselection of an unfavorable run.
- Generation development stayed CPU. Confirmation AE/decoders used MPS with differentiable CPU outputs; flows/evaluation stayed CPU. Both paired comparison arms share that change. Numerical parity of a probe does not imply identical optimization trajectories.
- The initial perception protocol incorrectly treated geometric zigzag half-turn symmetry as exact raster symmetry and trained pose modulo two. Original results remain visible. A direct raster audit, interpretation amendment, and a fresh binary/intensity crop study with full four-way pose supervision correct this issue. Supplemental old field names containing `unobservable` are explicitly withdrawn as an interpretation.
- Official SVIB data were downloaded from the project's public link. Only Hard dSprites Single Atomic was evaluated. Training is fixed at five epochs and final checkpoints; no test-driven stopping or suite-wide claim.
- Paired decoder reconstruction on fresh confirmation data is a post-hoc mechanism diagnostic; it did not alter the locked generation candidate. Full outputs and a fixed replay subset are retained.
- All performance conclusions distinguish completion of a comparison from achievement of a capability. Counterfactual, geometry, diversity and occlusion failures are retained. No paid external compute, upload, research-lab contact or new independent human review was performed.
- Runtime measurements reflect concurrent workloads. They support operational planning but are not controlled throughput benchmarks.
